Why Element Snapper?

Engineered for developers, designers, and QA engineers who need precision.

DPR-Aware Cropping

Handles Retina and 4K displays flawlessly. Coordinates are converted to physical pixels so your captures are never blurry.

Multi-Tile Stitching

Capturing an element larger than the viewport? No problem. The advanced stitching algorithm smoothly auto-scrolls and combines tiles perfectly.

Capture Delay

Set a countdown timer (0-10s) to easily capture hover states, dropdown menus, and dynamic UI elements before clipping.
