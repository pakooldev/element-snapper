# v1.6.0 (2026-11-21)

Mark up a capture before you share it, find your edits again later, and get cleaner stitched captures on pages that load, animate or zoom.

### ✨ New Features

- **Edit before you share**: The preview's new **Edit** button opens the capture in an editor. Crop it, draw boxes and arrows, add text, blur or black out private details, in any color (8 presets or your own), three sizes and solid, dashed or dotted lines. Paste, drop or pick more images from your capture history to put them together on one board. Everything can be selected, moved, resized and deleted, with Undo and Redo. Copy or download only what is inside the frame, using your format and quality settings.
- **My edits**: Every capture you change is saved on this computer as you go and listed under **My edits**, opened from the panel or the editor, until you delete it. Pressing Edit on the same capture again reopens your edit instead of starting over.
- **Step to the parent element**: While picking an element, press `↑` to highlight the element around the one under the pointer, and `↓` to go back. This reaches boxes that their child fills completely, which the mouse alone can't hover.

### 🎨 Improvements

- **Stitching measured from the screen**: Scroll Element and Page captures are now put together from what is actually visible in each tile instead of positions worked out ahead of time, so nested scroll boxes, app-style layouts with a scrolling main pane and pages that change while scrolling come out without repeats or misplaced tiles.
- **Waits for the page to settle**: Each tile now waits (up to 3 seconds) for images that are still loading and for fade-ins that start on scroll, and pauses looping animations such as spinners and CSS carousels, so every tile shows the same frame.
- **Works over modal dialogs**: The panel, highlight, countdown and preview now appear above an open modal dialog and can be clicked, instead of being hidden behind it.
- **The page stays out of selection**: Clicking to pick an element or dragging an area no longer clicks the button or link underneath.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **No gaps at browser zoom**: At zoom levels like 125% or 150%, captures of scrolling boxes could have one-pixel gaps between tiles. They now line up.
- **Copy falls back to a download**: When an image can't be copied (on `http://` pages, or when the page lost focus during a countdown), the capture is downloaded instead, with a note saying why, rather than being lost. Copy as Prompt now works on `http://` pages too.
